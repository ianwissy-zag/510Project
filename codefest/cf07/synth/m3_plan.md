My synthesis failed timing by 1ps on a 1650ps clock target. I consider this acceptable, and will not be making any changes to my design before m3. The critical path is part of my GELU computation, specifically passing through a BF16 multiplier and a BF16 to FP32 accumulator. This is the same path used in my systolic array, leading me to believe that if I had opted for an additional round of timing optimization, the timing on this path would have closed. I may resynthesize, but given the ~8 hours it takes to synthesize my design, I may opt to accept the timing failure, since it is so small. From my experience working with FPGA designs, timing failures much larger than this one compared to the clock period have resulted in successful hardware. I could also potentially just relax the constraint by one picosecond, which would have virtually no impact on the throughput of my design.
